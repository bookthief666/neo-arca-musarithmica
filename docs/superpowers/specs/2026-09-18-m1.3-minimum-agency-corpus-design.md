## 1. Purpose

M1.2.1R proved one source-backed computation, but it intentionally removed false choice. The next milestone must add **real agency**, not reintroduce decorative or scripted alternatives.

The roadmap consequence in the M1.2.1R design is binding:

> After M1.2.1R, add only enough independently verified column/row material to make multi-column arrangement and vertical selection musically real.

M1.3 therefore builds the smallest historical corpus in which:

1. more than one musarithmic source column is physically present;
2. each operative column has more than one independently verified Vperm row;
3. vertical displacement selects different actual historical pitch material;
4. multiple columns can be placed side-by-side as physical carriers;
5. the resulting symbolic music changes because the user changed historical source data, not because a UI state or seed changed.

This milestone is not the full Pinax IV transcription and not the full Arca corpus.

## 2. Historical operating grammar that M1.3 must honor

`docs/M0_6_PRIMARY_TEXT_CORRECTIONS.md` is the controlling physical reconstruction.

The relevant H0-T findings are:

- a printed pinax is a source table, not the manipulable object;
- individual musarithmic columns are copied onto separate wooden or paper rods/strips;
- multiple physical copies may be stored in the relevant receptacle;
- Syntagma-I physical columns should share equal row geometry;
- Kircher advises ten row positions for the physical columns;
- the operator removes needed columns, places them side-by-side, shifts them vertically, and reads the resulting aligned row bands;
- labelled cabinet cells are storage/indexing structures, not decorative bins.

The project must not regress to “tap one Pinax card and choose a row from a menu.”

## 3. Current corpus truth

At the start of M1.3, executable project-owned H0 data is deliberately narrow.

Canonical:

- Syntagma I / Pinax IV / source column I (“Stropha I”) / Vperm01;
- Pinax-IV duple Rperm03;
- printed-p.51 Tone II witness.

The existing Vperm01 is:

- Cantus `553233`
- Altus `875777`
- Tenor `323455`
- Bassus `858733`

The `868733` Bassus variant remains discrepancy history only.

The canonical Rperm03 remains:

`minim minim minim minim semibreve semibreve`

with derived relative-minim normalization:

`1 1 1 1 2 2`.

The current repository contains research staging and secondary-reference material for a much larger Pinax-IV corpus, but **that material is not executable H0** until it satisfies the project’s primary-witness protocol.

## 4. Minimum corpus definition

M1.3 targets exactly **two successive Pinax-IV source columns** and **two Vperm rows in each source column**.

Target source-column families:

- **Column I / Stropha I**
  - Vperm01 — already canonical;
  - Vperm02 — must be independently verified.
- **Column II / Stropha II**
  - Vperm01 — must be independently verified;
  - Vperm02 — must be independently verified.

Thus the milestone contains exactly four executable pitch permutations, three of them new.

### Why this is the minimum

Two source columns make side-by-side arrangement real.

Two row choices per source column make independent vertical selection real.

Together they provide four distinct pitch-material combinations:

`I.1 + II.1`
`I.1 + II.2`
`I.2 + II.1`
`I.2 + II.2`

No random seed is required to produce those differences.

### Rhythm scope

M1.3 keeps the already-verified duple Rperm03 fixed.

It does **not** add a rhythm selector merely to increase option count.

Rperm01 remains ambiguous and unavailable. A second rhythm row may be admitted only if it independently clears the same H0 verification protocol during the research pass and is explicitly added to a later design revision.

### Tone scope

Printed-p.51 Tone II remains the fixed witness-specific operating policy.

M1.3 does not create a tone-choice UI.

This isolates the milestone’s historical agency to the source-column/row mechanism that the project is trying to prove.

## 5. Source-verification protocol

No new Vperm value may enter executable data from a secondary transcription.

For each of the three new target Vperms:

1. perform Pass A from a public-domain 1650 primary image;
2. record every cell with an exact source locator;
3. perform Pass B against an independent 1650 witness;
4. require agreement for every one of the 24 pitch cells;
5. preserve any disagreement rather than resolving by majority or secondary authority;
6. only after the primary passes are recorded may a modern transcription be consulted as a cross-check.

Preferred primary witnesses already present in `historical_data/source_witnesses.json` include:

- ETH-Bibliothek Zürich / e-rara, Rar 610, Tomus II, printed p.83 = scan [92];
- BSB Munich, 2 Mus.th. 264-2;
- EPFL Library, `chepfl-lipr-AXC19_02`, 600-ppi;
- BnF/Gallica RES F-142.

The project may use a different independent public-domain 1650 copy if the witness ledger is updated first.

### Secondary material

Andrew A. Cashner’s digital Arca and Carlo Mario Chierotti’s study are useful **navigation and post-transcription cross-checks only**.

Cashner’s documentation confirms the structural interpretation that Pinax IV has four successive source columns and one shared Rperm table. It must not supply canonical project values before the primary passes.

## 6. Historical-data representation

Do not mutate or rename the existing canonical Vperm01 file because M0.9 depends on it.

Add three versioned records:

- `historical_data/syntagma1_pinax04/vperm02_printed_p83_verified.json`
- `historical_data/syntagma1_pinax04/stropha2_vperm01_printed_p83_verified.json`
- `historical_data/syntagma1_pinax04/stropha2_vperm02_printed_p83_verified.json`

Each record must carry:

- `canonical: true`;
- `verification_status: verified`;
- historical scope;
- exact four voice rows;
- source-column identity;
- Vperm identity;
- transcription protocol;
- at least two independent primary witness IDs/keys;
- complete 24-cell provenance;
- no editorial correction;
- discrepancies if any;
- explicit eligibility for the M1.3 agency corpus.

A new machine-readable agency manifest may index these records, but it must not duplicate pitch values.

## 7. Kernel architecture

M0.9 remains immutable.

Do **not** generalize `scripts/arca_historica_kernel.py` in place.

Create a new versioned executable layer, provisionally:

`scripts/arca_historica_agency_kernel.py`

It may borrow design patterns from M0.9 but must own its generalized source-column/Vperm validation.

The new kernel receives a server-resolved arrangement consisting of exactly two source-column selections:

- source column I with Vperm01 or Vperm02;
- source column II with Vperm01 or Vperm02.

It resolves both through:

- the fixed verified Rperm03;
- the fixed printed-p.51 Tone-II witness.

The canonical symbolic output is a two-segment historical arrangement:

- segment I = six events from source column I;
- segment II = six events from source column II;
- total = twelve symbolic events;
- each segment totals eight relative minim units;
- total arrangement = sixteen relative minim units.

Every event must preserve source-cell, rhythm-cell, tone-cell, source-column, and selected-Vperm provenance.

The kernel must fail closed for:

- unavailable source columns;
- unavailable Vperm rows;
- duplicate or missing required source columns;
- unverified records;
- witness disagreement;
- event-count mismatch;
- client-supplied pitch/rhythm/tone data;
- unsupported tone/rhythm choices.

## 8. Bridge contract

Introduce a new versioned bridge contract rather than silently widening v2.

Provisional formats:

- manifest: `neo-arca-mechanica-manifest/v3`
- request: `neo-arca-mechanica-reading/v3`
- execution: `neo-arca-mechanica-execution/v3`

The manifest exposes the **available authority**, not raw client-editable musical data.

For each of the two source columns it exposes:

- source-column ID;
- display/source label;
- permitted verified Vperm IDs;
- edition/content identity;
- row count = 2 for this milestone;
- H0/H1 authority notes.

The request sends only:

- manifest ID;
- content digest;
- two carrier instance IDs;
- their source-column IDs;
- their selected Vperm IDs;
- fixed slot/order identity.

The client does **not** send:

- pitch degree arrays;
- rhythm arrays;
- tone mapping;
- raw historical records;
- arbitrary row numbers not present in the manifest;
- a random seed.

The server resolves all historical content.

Unknown fields remain rejected.

## 9. Canonical reducer state

M1.3 introduces exactly two historical carrier instances.

Each carrier has:

- persistent `instance_id`;
- `source_column_id`;
- `edition_id`;
- location: cell / hand / workspace;
- selected verified Vperm ID.

The reducer owns musical agency:

- retrieve carrier I;
- retrieve carrier II;
- place into its fixed workbench slot;
- move carrier vertically between exactly two legal source-backed detents;
- execute the current arrangement;
- preserve both carrier identities and selections across error/retry/revelation/return.

Presentation owns:

- interpolation;
- camera;
- touch pointer ownership;
- hover/light;
- physical drag coordinates.

No musical state lives only inside R3F.

## 10. Physical workbench

Cell IV must now visibly contain two distinct source-column carriers, not duplicate copies of one source record.

They are labelled as successive Pinax-IV source columns.

The workbench contains two side-by-side operative slots:

`COLUMN I | COLUMN II`

Each rod has exactly two live vertical bands because exactly two rows are verified.

No additional dead bands may look selectable.

The physical rod face should visibly show the two source-backed Vperm blocks so that moving the rod changes which block aligns with the transverse reading rule.

### Vertical selection

Dragging a rod upward/downward changes its canonical selected Vperm.

The two rods move independently.

The user must be able to create all four combinations without opening Instrument Controls.

### Arrangement order

For this milestone, source-column order is fixed:

Column I precedes Column II.

Do not add drag-to-reorder until the corpus and historical use case justify it.

Side-by-side **placement** is real agency here; arbitrary reordering is not.

## 11. Transverse reading and causality

M1.2.1R correctly avoided calling its single-carrier event scrub “transverse reading.”

M1.3 may introduce true transverse-reading language only where the physical display actually spans the two arranged column rods.

A transverse rule should show:

- which verified Vperm row is active on Column I;
- which verified Vperm row is active on Column II;
- the fixed Rperm03 shared by both;
- the fixed Tone-II mapping used to resolve each degree;
- the resulting segment I and segment II symbolic events.

The user should understand before LECTIO that changing either rod changes only the segment sourced from that rod.

LECTIO consolidates/validates the already-visible arrangement; it does not secretly choose music.

## 12. Semantic/accessibility parity

Instrument Controls remain a peer over the same reducer, not a separate workflow.

The semantic surface must expose:

- both carrier identities and locations;
- both source-column labels;
- the two verified Vperm choices for each;
- exact selected four-voice degree rows;
- fixed Rperm03;
- fixed Tone II;
- twelve-event result structure;
- provenance;
- LECTIO/error/retry/return.

Internal Vperm choice should be represented by stable source IDs, not display index alone.

Keyboard and screen-reader operation dispatch the same canonical actions as direct manipulation.

## 13. Fold-first interaction

The M1.2.1R physical lesson is binding:

> A technically present mesh is not an acceptable direct-manipulation control if the owner cannot discover or grab it.

For both rods:

- the visible rod body itself is a generous drag surface;
- no tiny finial-only hotspot is required;
- touch ownership suppresses orbit during rod manipulation;
- release/cancel always returns orbit ownership;
- Fold-sized mobile-touch emulation is a continuous CI gate;
- real Fold testing is a release/freeze smoke test under the owner’s explicit continuation decision.

The semantic panel remains available, but successful sighted operation must not depend on it.

## 14. Error behavior

If either source column or selected Vperm cannot be validated:

- no historical execution is produced;
- the physical arrangement remains visible;
- both carrier selections remain intact;
- the error identifies the unavailable/mismatched source identity;
- retry does not reset the cabinet.

If only one new Vperm passes research verification, **shrink the milestone** rather than inventing the fourth choice.

Do not substitute secondary values to satisfy the planned 2×2 matrix.

## 15. Performance constraints

Preserve:

- lazy-loaded spatial renderer;
- legacy fallback;
- procedural geometry;
- deterministic compact textures;
- no new physics/postprocessing/Blender dependency;
- generated material/texture disposal;
- no per-frame allocation in drag/read paths.

M1.3 should not increase the M1.2.1R `SpatialArca` gzip chunk by more than 10% without a recorded justification.

## 16. Explicit non-goals

M1.3 does not add:

- all ten Vperm rows;
- all four Pinax-IV source columns;
- a second tone;
- a new rhythm choice unless separately approved after H0 verification;
- Syntagma II or III;
- octave/register realization;
- ficta automation;
- Ghost/Neo composition;
- audio, MIDI, or Tone.js;
- text setting;
- random generation;
- locks/mutation;
- WebXR;
- accounts/save/deployment;
- photoreal asset production.

## 17. Acceptance gate

M1.3 passes only when:

1. the three new Vperm records are independently verified H0 project data;
2. M0.9 output remains byte/structure-equivalent under its existing tests;
3. the new kernel exposes exactly the four intended pitch combinations;
4. two physical source-column carriers exist simultaneously;
5. each carrier has exactly two truthful vertical selections;
6. all four combinations are reachable through direct manipulation;
7. changing Column I changes segment I without silently changing Column II;
8. changing Column II changes segment II without silently changing Column I;
9. fixed Rperm03 and Tone II remain visibly causal;
10. no semantic-control-only success path is required for a sighted operator;
11. source/result provenance survives through execution/error/retry/return;
12. complete automated, mobile-touch, accessibility, and performance gates pass;
13. real-device owner smoke testing remains explicitly recorded as passed, failed, or deferred—never inferred.

## 18. Roadmap after M1.3

Only after the 2×2 historical agency matrix is comprehensible should the project decide among:

- expanding to all four Pinax-IV source columns;
- adding more verified Vperm rows;
- adding a second independently verified Rperm choice;
- beginning the spatial craft/material-fidelity pass;
- opening a narrow N1/Neo instrument bridge.

The decision should be driven by what the 2×2 instrument teaches, not by a desire to maximize option count.
