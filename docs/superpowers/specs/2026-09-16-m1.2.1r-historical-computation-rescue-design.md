# M1.2.1R Historical Computation Rescue — Design

**Date:** 2026-09-17

**Repository:** `bookthief666/neo-arca-musarithmica`

**Design branch:** `feature/m1.2.1r-historical-computation-rescue-design`

**Accepted implementation base:** `feature/m1.2-spatial-arca-prototype` at `7dc5e3c66e4602478d4f8a23f45f136db457f0e1`

**Status:** peer-review correction; revised design authority awaiting delta review

**Supersedes as implementation direction:** the three-carrier comprehension flow designed on `feature/m1.2.1-spatial-comprehension` and implemented at rejected checkpoint `ccda572849e15c60dde5d6d8f6f7d8f65bde088d`

## 1. Decision

M1.2.1R will replace the rejected fixed three-carrier ritual with a truthful,
source-classified **single-fragment historical computation workbench**.

The milestone will show, at operating scale, how the verified Pinax IV pitch
permutation, verified Rperm03 rhythm, and printed-p.51 Tone II witness combine to
produce the M0.9 symbolic four-voice fragment. It will not pretend that the
current corpus offers multiple verified columns or rows when it does not.

The accepted M1.2 spatial architecture survives. The rejected candidate remains
an implementation checkpoint from which individually reviewed mechanisms may be
ported. It is not the implementation base and will not be fast-forwarded into the
canonical M1.2.1 branch.

This milestone is a comprehension and operative-truth rescue, not a craft pass,
corpus-expansion pass, or XR pass.

## 2. Why the previous design failed

The M1.2 bridge currently requires three visible carrier instances:

1. a copy of `S1.P4.STROPHA1.VPERM01`;
2. a second copy of the same Vperm;
3. a separate carrier for `S1.P4.NOTAE_TEMPORIS.DUPLE.RPERM03`.

All three must expose band I. The bridge then calls the same deterministic M0.9
fragment builder.

That arrangement is internally consistent but experientially false:

- the duplicate Vperm rods do not contribute distinct musical information;
- separating rhythm onto a dedicated rod is not the documented historical
  column structure;
- bands II–X imply a choice even though they are untranscribed;
- alignment changes execution eligibility but not the selected music;
- Tone II and LECTIO behave as state gates rather than visible transformations;
- derived next-affordance guidance teaches a sequence of taps, not the
  computation.

The owner therefore could advance the machine without understanding why the
actions mattered. The nonzero-carriage drag defect made the problem worse, but
fixing that defect alone would preserve the failed operative model.

## 3. Product outcome

At the end of M1.2.1R, a first-time sighted operator on the Samsung Galaxy Z
Fold 6 must be able to explain:

1. the virga carries a source-backed musical-number fragment;
2. the Vperm supplies four simultaneous scale degrees at each event position;
3. Rperm03 supplies six verified mensural duration identities, from which the
   project derives the operative relative-minim normalization;
4. Tone II maps each scale degree to a witness-specific pitch class;
5. the final folio is the visible consequence of those three inputs;
6. untranscribed corpus capacity is visibly unavailable, not presented as
   selectable musical content.

Completion without that explanation is not acceptance.

## 4. Historical evidence and operative model

### 4.1 Primary and scholarly authority

The governing external sources are:

- Athanasius Kircher, *Musurgia universalis* (Rome, 1650), Book VIII,
  *Musurgia mirifica*, Part V, especially the construction and use chapters:
  <https://archive.org/details/athanasiikircherkirc>;
- Andrew A. Cashner, “Athanasius Kircher’s Arca musarithmica (1650) as a
  Computational System,” *Journal of Creative Music Systems* 8/1 (2024),
  DOI `10.5920/jcms.1325`;
- Cashner’s operational overview and digital reconstruction:
  <https://www.arca1650.info/about.html>.

Project-owned authority remains:

- `docs/M0_6_PRIMARY_TEXT_CORRECTIONS.md`;
- `docs/HISTORICAL_DATA_TRANSCRIPTION_SPEC.md`;
- `docs/M0_9_ARCA_HISTORICA_KERNEL.md`;
- the verified JSON records under `historical_data/`;
- `scripts/arca_historica_kernel.py`.

When an earlier project interpretation conflicts with a primary witness or a
better-supported scholarly reconstruction, the discrepancy must be recorded and
the stronger evidence governs.

### 4.2 Documented historical sequence

The documented historical sequence is:

1. prepare a syllabified text, with the quantities or metrical information
   required by the selected procedure;
2. select style/Syntagma, tone, mensuration, and the appropriate Pinax;
3. retrieve repeated copies of individual columns appropriate to the ordered
   text segments or verse lines;
4. arrange the column carriers side by side;
5. move the carriers vertically and read a transverse series;
6. in Syntagma I, select pitch and rhythm permutations; in Syntagma II, use the
   prescribed pitch/rhythm pairing;
7. use the tone table to translate degrees into note names;
8. write the result on four staves, selecting clefs/ranges and making the
   judgments Kircher leaves to the operator.

The historically meaningful physical unit is an individual musarithmic column
copied from a printed Pinax onto a wooden or paper virga. A whole Pinax is not
the movable unit.

### 4.3 What was physical and what was conceptual

Historically physical:

- the Arca as storage cabinet;
- labeled cells and covers;
- repeated paper or wooden column carriers;
- carrier retrieval and side-by-side arrangement;
- vertical displacement of carriers;
- transverse visual reading.

Historically conceptual or manually executed:

- preparing and marking the text;
- choosing the proper Syntagma, Pinax, tone, and mensuration;
- selecting Vperm/Rperm material according to the procedure;
- looking up degree-to-pitch mappings;
- writing on staves;
- setting clefs and ranges;
- applying octaves, ficta, and other corrections where required.

There is no present evidence that Kircher specified the project’s pullout
carriage, optical reader, Tone-II knob, or LECTIO lever as mechanical devices.
They are H1 explanatory mechanisms and must never be silently presented as H0.

## 6. Data truth and critical-edition carrier

### 6.1 The available data

M1.2.1R has exactly:

- one verified Vperm:
  - Cantus `553233`;
  - Altus `875777`;
  - Tenor `323455`;
  - Bassus `858733`;
- one verified Rperm:
  - H0 source sequence: minim, minim, minim, minim, semibreve, semibreve;
  - derived project normalization: relative minim units `1,1,1,1,2,2`;
- one verified printed-p.51 Tone II mapping;
- one deterministic six-event, eight-relative-minim output.

This is enough to teach a computation. It is not enough to represent a freely
combinatorial instrument.

### 6.2 One carrier, two source fields

The active virga is an H1 **critical-edition carrier** containing two clearly
separated H0 source fields selected editorially from the currently verified
Pinax-IV/Syntagma-I material:

- `VOCES / VPERM 01`: four labeled rows of six degrees;
- `NOTAE TEMPORIS / RPERM 03`: six H0 mensural duration identities, with the
  operative `1,1,1,1,2,2` ratio separately labeled as a derived project
  normalization.

The two records are not claimed to form an intrinsic or uniquely prescribed
historical pair. Their conjunction is the M0.9 project's bounded editorial
selection. The carrier references the immutable records rather than copying
musical authority into TypeScript. The bridge remains responsible for building
the manifest.

The carrier must visibly say that it is an extract from Pinax IV rather than a
complete transcribed historical column.

### 6.3 Untranscribed material and dormant capacity

Bands II–X do not exist as selectable values in M1.2.1R canonical state,
requests, drag detents, pointer targets, or accessibility controls. The active
carrier has one verified reading pose.

The cabinet may retain visibly sealed corpus capacity so the object still reads
as part of a larger machine. That capacity must be inert construction labeled as
unavailable editorial territory, not nine equivalent-looking musical choices.
The exact three-channel workbench geometry remains H1.

## 7. Experience design

### 7.1 Persistent object and spatial framing

The experience remains one coherent Arca in one R3F scene. There is no modal DOM
wizard and no replacement instructional screen.

The camera has two presentation regimes:

- **reliquary inspection:** enough distance to understand the whole cabinet;
- **operative workbench:** a close, stable view of Cell IV, the carriage, active
  carrier, reader, Mensa, and working folio.

Opening Cell IV and seating the carrier transitions toward the operative
workbench. Camera state is still presentation-only. The user retains orbit/pinch
control; automatic framing may not continually fight the operator.

### 7.2 Physical flow

1. Open the Arca.
2. Bring Bank I and Cell IV forward.
3. Open Cell IV’s cover.
4. Lift the single critical-edition carrier.
5. Carry and seat that same instance in the active channel.
6. Inspect its Vperm and Rperm fields at macro scale.
7. The carrier seats directly in its sole verified reading pose.
8. The printed-p.51 Tone II witness is visibly identified as the fixed operative
   map; manipulating its H1 control reveals or routes the mapping but does not
   unlock execution.
9. Scrub a six-position event reader freely across the fragment.
10. At each position, see four degrees map to four pitch classes while the
    corresponding duration is attached.
11. Invoke LECTIO to open the completed symbolic folio.
12. Return to the intact working arrangement without redeploying the carrier.

This is not a locked tutorial. The event reader may move backward or forward,
Tone II may be inspected at any time after the carrier is seated, and no hidden
“watched every step” flag controls execution.

### 7.3 Immediate causal feedback

At each of the six positions the reader shows:

| Layer | Example at event 1 |
|---|---|
| Source Vperm degrees | C `5`, A `8`, T `3`, B `8` |
| Tone-II lookup | `5→D`, `8→G`, `3→Bb`, `8→G` |
| Rperm duration | H0 mensural identity: minim; derived normalization: `1` relative minim unit |
| Accumulated symbolic event | C `D`, A `G`, T `Bb`, B `G`, duration `1` |

The complete transformation is visible without opening provenance. Provenance
adds witness paths and editorial detail; it does not contain the explanation
that the main instrument omitted.

### 7.4 Event reader and LECTIO

The event reader becomes an H1 magnifying inspection lens:

- it magnifies the active Vperm column position and matching Rperm symbol;
- four restrained traces connect degrees to the Tone-II table;
- resolved pitch classes appear adjacent to the source degrees;
- a six-position index communicates progress through the fragment;
- the user can scrub directly rather than repeatedly tap “next.”

This six-position intra-column operation is not historical transverse reading,
which requires several arranged columns and may include vertical displacement.

LECTIO is enabled when the carrier is seated, its edition identity is valid,
and no execution is pending. It may be invoked without scrubbing all six
positions, because comprehension is not enforced through hidden tutorial state.
The revealed folio still presents the same source-to-result trace so skipping
inspection never hides causality.

### 7.5 Guidance

`getNextAffordance()` survives as a derived recovery/highlight facility, not as
the main explanatory system.

Guidance may answer:

- what object is operable now;
- why an unavailable action did nothing;
- how to recover from an interrupted gesture or rejected edition/result.

It must not reduce the experience to imperative sentences such as “tap this,
then tap that.” Labels should name musical meaning: `VOCES`, `NOTAE TEMPORIS`,
`TONVS II`, `GRADVS`, `SONVS`, and `DVRATIO`, with concise English equivalents
in the semantic layer.

## 8. Canonical state and data contracts

### 8.1 State ownership

The existing reducer remains the sole authority for:

- cabinet/bank/cell state;
- active carrier identity and location;
- event-reader position;
- execution lifecycle and result;
- error/recovery state;
- provenance visibility and reduced motion.

The renderer owns only interpolation, camera pose, hover, illumination intensity,
and other ephemeral presentation state.

### 8.2 Manifest version

The operative contract changes materially, so the manifest must become
`neo-arca-mechanica-manifest/v2`; v1 must not be silently reinterpreted.

The v2 manifest contains:

```text
manifest_id
content_digest
critical_edition_carrier
  carrier_id
  edition_id
  label
  classification: H1
  pitch_source
    source_column_id
    immutable_record
    provenance_paths
  rhythm_source
    source_column_id
    immutable_record
    provenance_paths
tone
  number
  name
  system
  witness
  witness_classification: H0
  operating_policy_classification: H1
  known_conflict
  degree_to_pitch_class
limits
```

The TypeScript client may render this payload but may not manufacture historical
rows, rhythms, mappings, or provenance.

### 8.3 Execution request

Replace the misleading three-instance alignment request with a versioned reading
request:

```text
format: neo-arca-mechanica-reading/v2
manifest_id
content_digest
carrier_instance
  instance_id
  carrier_id
  edition_id
  location: workspace
```

The event-reader position is not sent as historical input because it changes
only which already-selected event the operator is inspecting. The fixed Tone-II policy is
also resolved by the authoritative manifest rather than accepted as a
client-controlled choice. The bridge strictly rejects unknown request fields,
validates the manifest/edition/content identity and workspace carrier, resolves
source records server-side, and fingerprints the normalized validated request
before invoking the unchanged M0.9 kernel.

The execution envelope must likewise become
`neo-arca-mechanica-execution/v2`. Its `reading` field records the validated
carrier instance, manifest/edition/content identity, applied Tone-II
number/witness, and the classification `H0-backed content through H1
critical-edition carrier`. It must not retain the v1
three-rod `arrangement` shape. The nested
`neo-arca-historica-symbolic/v1` fragment remains byte-for-byte equivalent to
the M0.9 kernel output; its historical schema is not versioned merely because
the physical request envelope changed.

### 8.4 Reducer vocabulary

The implementation plan should express these canonical operations without
creating a second state machine:

- `RETRIEVE_CARRIER`;
- `PLACE_HELD_CARRIER`;
- `SET_READING_POSITION`;
- `EXECUTE`;
- existing success/error/return/provenance/reduced-motion actions.

The migration must explicitly replace obsolete `InstrumentPhase`,
`InstrumentAffordance`, `deriveInstrumentView()`, and
`getNextAffordance()` semantics rather than leaving rod-alignment or
Tone-unlock states reachable. The data model must describe exactly one
critical-edition carrier for this milestone. Object identity must persist from
storage to hand to workstation.

### 8.5 Candidate salvage policy

Eligible for selective port from `ccda572…` after review:

- one-held-object reducer guard;
- stored → held → seated object identity;
- folding Cell IV cover and visible mortises;
- destination-gated placement;
- camera ownership and reduced intervention;
- accessible-control parity;
- error/retry continuity;
- touch-size refinements;
- persistent Tone-II and LECTIO mechanisms only where Tone II reveals the fixed
  mapping rather than serving as an unlock gate.

Explicitly rejected from that checkpoint:

- two duplicate Vperm carriers plus a separate Rperm carrier;
- mandatory three-channel deployment;
- all-three-at-band-I readiness;
- guidance-led scripted completion;
- Tone II and LECTIO as unexplained unlock gates;
- any presentation that makes sealed bands look like equivalent valid choices.

## 9. Direct-manipulation repair

### 9.1 Coordinate-space defect

The accepted M1.2 drag code constructs a world-space channel origin containing
the carriage’s live `z` travel, but also adds that travel to local
`VIRGA_TRAVEL` limits and subtracts it from the projected scalar afterward.

`projectRayOntoAxis()` returns the scalar parameter relative to its supplied
origin. Once the origin contains the parent translation, its min/max bounds must
remain in that local scalar frame. Carriage travel must not be applied again.

### 9.2 Required debugging sequence

M1.2.1R no longer requires carrier-band sliding, because no alternate verified
bands exist. The coordinate-space defect must nevertheless be captured and
removed while extracting the reusable translated-axis detent helper used by the
six-position event reader. Implementation must follow this order:

1. add a regression reproducing projection with a nonzero translated carriage;
2. demonstrate that the existing calculation selects the wrong detent;
3. extract a pure `ray + translated world origin + local bounds -> detent`
   function that fails under the old arithmetic and passes under the corrected
   contract;
4. verify zero and nonzero carriage positions;
5. verify first/last/internal detents, parallel rays, pointer cancel, unmount,
   pointer release off-mesh, and orbit suppression;
6. run all automated gates;
7. perform trusted touch QA on the physical Galaxy Z Fold 6.

Gesture ownership must move out of `document.body.dataset.arcaGrab` into a
presentation-owned ref/context shared by the scene and constrained controls.
No physical success may be claimed from automated tests alone.

## 10. Accessibility

The semantic `Instrument Controls` layer remains a parallel surface over the
same reducer and bridge.

It must expose:

- the active carrier and whether it is stored, held, or seated;
- four labeled Vperm rows;
- Rperm glyphs identified as H0 source material and relative minim units
  identified as their derived project normalization;
- the complete Tone-II witness-specific mapping;
- a six-position reading control;
- the four degree-to-pitch transformations at the current position;
- LECTIO and the resulting symbolic folio;
- provenance and all non-claims.

Keyboard and screen-reader operation dispatch the same canonical actions as the
3D object. The semantic layer owns no musical state and does not become the
primary sighted interface.

Reduced-motion mode preserves every data transformation while removing or
shortening camera and object interpolation.

## 11. Error and recovery behavior

- **Manifest unavailable:** retain legacy fallback and show a concise semantic
  failure; do not construct placeholder historical data.
- **Edition or content mismatch:** reject execution without clearing the
  physical arrangement; require a fresh authoritative manifest before retry.
- **Bridge failure:** preserve carrier identity and reading position for
  retry; no full cabinet reset.
- **Pointer cancel/lost release:** terminate the grab, clear orbit suppression,
  and snap to the nearest legal physical detent.
- **Missing source or mapping cell:** fail closed. Never infer a pitch, rhythm,
  octave, ficta, or provenance path.

## 12. Testing and verification

### 12.1 Python/bridge

- manifest v2 contains exactly the verified Vperm, Rperm03 mensural identities,
  and Tone-II data, while labeling relative-minim integers as a derived
  normalization;
- v1 and v2 contracts are not confused;
- carrier, edition, and content identities are immutable;
- unknown fields, stale v1 payloads, and mismatched editions are rejected;
- Tone II is resolved from the manifest rather than accepted as a client choice;
- exact M0.9 output and provenance remain unchanged;
- Bassus `858733` remains canonical and the discrepancy remains in provenance;
- Rperm01 remains unavailable.

### 12.2 Reducer/client

- one held carrier maximum;
- same instance persists through cell → hand → workspace;
- exactly one carrier exists; contradictory extra carriers fail closed;
- unavailable actions are no-ops with no hidden progress;
- reducer input clamps the reading position to the six actual events while the
  historical selector rejects impossible canonical positions;
- reading position does not alter the historical request;
- return from revelation preserves the working arrangement;
- error/retry preserves state.

### 12.3 Spatial interaction

- translated-carriage regression fails before the fix and passes after it;
- all six event-reader detents are reachable at zero and nonzero carriage travel;
- pointer-up and pointer-cancel end grabs;
- dragging never orbits the camera;
- orbit/pinch resumes after release;
- no duplicate carrier appears during transfer;
- active-grab cleanup survives cancel, lost release, and unmount.

### 12.4 Accessibility

- full keyboard operation;
- screen-reader names and states for the carrier, mapping, cursor, and
  LECTIO;
- semantic values exactly match the spatial values and bridge output;
- focus remains stable through execution and retry.
- a real screen-reader smoke test is recorded in addition to automated ARIA
  assertions.

### 12.5 Physical Fold acceptance

Required on the owner’s Galaxy Z Fold 6:

- portrait and unfolded browser layouts;
- readable Vperm/Rperm data without browser zoom;
- reliable lift, place, event-reader scrub, Tone-II inspection, and LECTIO
  gestures;
- no accidental camera orbit during object manipulation;
- no tiny hidden hotspot required for progression;
- operator can explain the computation in their own words;
- operator distinguishes source-backed data from reconstructed mechanics;
- operator does not progress mainly by random tapping.

The owner’s verdict is the acceptance authority for this milestone.

## 13. Performance constraints

- Preserve lazy loading of the spatial renderer and the legacy fallback.
- Do not add Blender assets, a physics engine, postprocessing stack, or new
  full-resolution texture atlas.
- Prefer existing procedural geometry and compact deterministic labels.
- Do not increase the accepted M1.2 spatial chunk by more than 10 percent without
  an explicit recorded justification; record both raw and compressed transfer
  size from an unambiguous build-manifest chunk identity.
- Resolve or account for multiple-Three-instance warnings before release.
- Dispose generated textures/materials correctly and avoid per-frame allocation
  in the reading traces.
- Visual texture randomness must not make screenshot QA irreproducible.

## 14. Explicit non-goals

M1.2.1R does not add:

- additional historical Vperm/Rperm transcriptions;
- Syntagma II or III;
- historical text entry or automatic syllabification;
- octave/register realization;
- ficta automation;
- audio, MIDI, Tone.js, or playback;
- the Ghost or an `instrument` bridge into it;
- Neo-Arca or Modus Haereticus behavior;
- photoreal materials or production Blender assets;
- physics;
- WebXR session lifecycle, controllers, or hand tracking;
- save/accounts/deployment work;
- a tutorial-memory subsystem.
- repository-default-branch governance changes; the stale default remains
  separately recorded debt requiring an owner decision.

## 15. Acceptance gate

M1.2.1R is accepted only when all of the following are true:

1. the authority contract above is reflected in the runtime manifest and visible
   object;
2. the three-carrier fixed ritual is gone;
3. the M0.9 kernel and source records remain unchanged;
4. the translated-axis defect has an integrated regression test and the event
   reader passes physical touch verification;
5. Vperm, Rperm, Tone II, and resulting events are legibly and visibly causal;
6. sighted and semantic interfaces use one canonical state and one bridge;
7. all automated gates pass;
8. the production build remains within the recorded performance constraints;
9. physical Fold QA passes;
10. the owner accepts comprehension, not merely completion.

## 16. Roadmap consequence

After M1.2.1R acceptance, the next milestone is not immediately the full material
pass. It is a bounded **Minimum Agency Corpus**: only enough independently
verified column/row material to make multi-column arrangement and vertical
selection musically real.

Once that bounded corpus supports genuine choices and the same workbench remains
comprehensible, the project may proceed to spatial craft/material fidelity. The
broader corpus, Neo/Haeretica evolution, audio, and WebXR follow later.

This ordering prevents beautiful production assets from hardening an
experientially false machine.
